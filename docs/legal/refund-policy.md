# Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is the same policy published at `/refund`.

Files are delivered as soon as payment is verified, so refunds are for delivery and billing problems.

Ask within 7 days of payment if:

- money was taken but the order never became paid and the file cannot be downloaded
- the file is corrupt, empty, or is a different product
- the same product was charged twice

A refund is not offered after a working file has been downloaded, for a change of mind, or because the notes did not produce a particular result.

Approved refunds go back through Razorpay to the original method. An order marked refunded loses download access, because downloads require status `paid`.

Contact the store with the account email, product name, and Razorpay payment id.
