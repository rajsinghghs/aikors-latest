# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This is the same policy published at `/privacy`.

AIkors sells digital study material in INR through Razorpay. Accounts use a name, email, and hashed password. Google sign-in is optional and stores the profile Google returns for that login (name and email).

The store keeps orders, Razorpay payment references (order id, payment id, signature, amount, currency, status, method), reviews, and download access. It does not store full card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

The browser stores `cv_token` and `cv_user` in local storage so the session survives a refresh. That is not an advertising cookie.

Password reset emails are sent through the configured SMTP server. The link expires after 15 minutes.

Thumbnails and preview screenshots may be public on Cloudflare R2. Paid files are private and are served only after a paid order is confirmed.

Data is used to run accounts, checkout, delivery, reviews, and support. It is shared with Razorpay, with Google if you choose that login, with Cloudflare R2 when storage is configured, and with the email provider. It is not sold.

You can ask for a correction or for account deletion using the support email in the footer, or from the email on your account if no public address is set. Order records may be kept where a payment dispute requires them.

The site is not directed at children under 13.
